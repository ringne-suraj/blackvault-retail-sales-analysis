# blackvault-retail-sales-analysis
End-to-end retail sales analysis using SQL and Power BI, covering data quality, business performance, customer, product, regional and shipping analysis.


## 📌 Project Overview

BlackVault is an end-to-end retail sales analytics project built to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights.

The project follows a complete analytics workflow — from data quality assessment and cleaning to SQL-based business analysis and interactive Power BI reporting.

The analysis focuses on understanding sales performance, profitability, product and regional performance, customer behavior, time-based trends, and shipping-related patterns.


## 🎯 Business Problem

Retail businesses generate large volumes of transactional data, but raw data alone does not provide clear answers to important business questions.

The objective of this project is to analyze retail sales data and identify patterns in sales, profit, products, customers, regions, time periods, and shipping performance.

The analysis also includes a data quality assessment to identify and address issues before performing business analysis, ensuring that the final insights are based on reliable and consistent data.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Assess and improve the quality of the raw datasets.
- Identify missing, duplicate, invalid, and inconsistent data.
- Analyze overall sales and profitability performance.
- Identify high- and low-performing products and categories.
- Analyze regional and market-level performance.
- Identify important time-based sales trends.
- Analyze shipping performance and patterns.
- Use SQL to perform structured business analysis.
- Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard to communicate key findings.
- Generate actionable business insights and recommendations.

## 📂 Dataset

The project uses three related datasets from a retail sales database:

| Dataset | Description |
|---|---|
| **Orders** | Transaction-level sales data containing information about orders, customers, products, sales, profit, shipping, and related attributes. |
| **People** | Regional and manager-related information used to support geographical analysis. |
| **Returns** | Information about returned orders used to analyze return-related patterns. |

The datasets were examined and prepared for data quality issues before being used for business analysis and dashboard development.
